## What is being assessed
The current V7 engineering definition is supplied for a focused technical assessment of packaging, occupant interfaces, seatback geometry/motion, clearance, rail and seat-to-body integration, BIW dependencies, kinematic interactions, mechanical interfaces, DFM/DFA observations, missing engineering inputs, and justified proposed corrections.

## What the supplied definition can support
The external engineer can directly inspect the V7-R3 STEP geometry and the disclosed model-level relationships among the architecture components. The current disclosed kinematic reference contains **2 MODEL_LEVEL generalized coordinates**, a **0–180 mm MODEL_LEVEL q1 range**, and a **-10° to +25° MODEL_LEVEL q2 range**. A **25° CONCEPT_LEVEL nominal seatback angle** and **420 mm CONCEPT_LEVEL rail-spacing reference** are available for context only.

These values do not establish physical capability, vehicle installation, or manufacturing release.

## Critical dependencies
Authoritative vehicle datum, H/R-point definition, vehicle seat hardpoints, restraint anchors, BIW geometry, occupant package/envelope, released tolerances/GD&T, material/fastener definitions, and physical performance data remain required where absent. Mass properties are not established unless separately controlled evidence is supplied.

## Required posture
The assessment must be evidence-led and fail-closed: geometry observations may be recorded; model-level kinematics may be discussed as model-level; vehicle-dependent conclusions remain conditional; missing information is a finding; and no unsupported crash, CAE, safety, regulatory, or manufacturing claim is acceptable.
